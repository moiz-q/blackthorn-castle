Send your pages here and they're turned into the comic for you: same size and clean edges as the rest, with a place in the reader and on the cover.

  1. Get your pages ready

    One image per page, in PNG, JPG or WebP. Name them by page number (1.png, 2.png, 3.png …) so the order is clear. Wide 3:2 pages, such as 1536×1024, match the rest of the comic. Each image must be under 10 MB.

  2. Check them here

    Pick all the pages at once. They stay on your device, and nothing is uploaded.

      1. Send them

        The chapter form opens on GitHub, where the comic is kept. You'll need a free GitHub account. Fill in the chapter number, then drag your pages into the box in page order: 1 first, then 2, and so on.

        Open the chapter form

      2. Check the result

        In a few minutes a reply shows every page, numbered, exactly as it will appear. If something's out of order, edit your post and the pages are rebuilt. Once the chapter is approved it goes live here.
